# ADS Alive, Cluster and Pin 7 Research

**Date:** 2026-03-23
**Session ID:** d936150d-0b2b-4c2c-aee8-ff2e0ffe14e6

## Summary

Long technical session on ADS I diagnostics. Confirmed the ADS module wakes with
>13 V applied. Pin 9 gave one blink (no stored faults). Clarified that ADS I is
adaptive damping only, not self-leveling, for this car.

Cluster investigation went through several iterations: initially thought the ADS
warning lamp was missing, then pivoted to ABS research. Deep dive into X11 Pin 7
determined it is the roll bar (RB) diagnostic output, not ABS, on this 16-pin
connector car. Concluded that ABS blink codes are not available on X11 for early
R129 -- the ABS warning lamp on the cluster is the primary diagnostic indicator.
User photos confirmed the ABS symbol is present on the cluster, so the "missing
strip" theory was rolled back. Roll bar faults noted vs. working mechanism.
