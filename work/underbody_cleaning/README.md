# Underbody Cleaning, Inspection & Protection — Summer 2026

**Vehicle:** 1991 Mercedes-Benz 500 SL (R129) | JNY-315
**Priority:** LOW — summer vacation project
**Prerequisite:** Car on jack stands or lift, warm dry weather

---

## Background

Underbody inspected during katsastus (2026-04-02). Generally clean (summer-only Swedish car, ~340 km/year for 16 years), but 35 years of accumulated road grime. Specific areas need attention:

- **Rear differential housing:** Surface rust
- **Exhaust center silencer ("xbox"):** Outer shell starting to rust, inner shell OK
- **Factory undercoating:** Condition unknown in detail — needs full inspection
- **Bare metal areas:** Behind front wheels (noted earlier), any stone-chip spots

## Approach

### 1. Gentle Wash (NOT pressure wash)

- Garden hose only, not Kärcher at close range
- Soften accumulated dirt with APC spray (diluted), let it dwell 5 min, rinse
- Work section by section: front subframe, floor pans, transmission tunnel, rear axle
- **DO NOT pressure-wash near undercoating edges** — risk of blasting under delaminated coating and trapping moisture

### 2. Inspect Factory Undercoating

While the underside is clean and wet:

- [ ] Tap all coated areas with a screwdriver — solid = good, hollow = delaminated
- [ ] Mark delaminated zones with chalk or tape
- [ ] Look for cracks, lifting edges, or exposed metal underneath
- [ ] Photograph each area for the record
- [ ] **Intact coating = leave it alone.** Do not "refresh" with spray-over — new product won't bond to old PVC/bitumen and creates moisture traps

### 3. Differential Housing — Rust Treatment

| Step | Action | Product |
|------|--------|---------|
| 1 | Wire-brush surface rust (hand brush or drill attachment) | Wire wheel / cup brush |
| 2 | Clean with brake cleaner | On hand |
| 3 | Apply rust converter | Fertan, Brunox Epoxy, or Hammerite Kurust |
| 4 | Let cure per product instructions (usually 24h) | — |
| 5 | Apply protective coating | Tectyl 506 cavity wax, or Dinitrol ML spray |

### 4. Exhaust System — Monitor Only

- Outer shell rust is cosmetic and inevitable at this age
- **Do NOT coat** — exhaust temperatures (300–600°C) will burn off any paint/coating, and the fumes are toxic
- Monitor the inner shell for perforation at each service interval
- If outer shell perforates, assess whether the silencer needs replacement vs. the inner shell still holding

### 5. Bare Metal / Stone Chip Areas

- [ ] Behind front wheels — touch up with 744 Brilliant Silver Metallic paint pen + clear coat (already on parts list)
- [ ] Any other exposed steel found during inspection — clean, prime (zinc-rich primer), top coat
- [ ] Wheel arches — inspect for hidden rust under plastic arch liners (remove liners if accessible)

### 6. Cavity Wax (Optional — High Value)

If motivated, inject cavity wax (Tectyl 506 or Dinitrol ML) into box sections and hollow members via existing drain holes:
- Sills / rocker panels
- Door bottoms
- Trunk floor channels
- Front fender inner structure

This is the single highest-value rust prevention step for a 35-year-old car that will see Finnish weather, but requires access to drain/access holes and a spray wand. Research R129-specific access points before attempting.
