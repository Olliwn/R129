## Saturday — Car on stands: under-car cluster

Order chosen to minimise dropped-tool-in-drained-fluid risk.

### 1. M119 oiler tube tap/wipe test — ENGINE STONE-COLD

- Full procedure: `work/m119_upper_timing/README.md` §2.3
- Pass = bright *ping* (metal) or silver streak on wipe (aluminum) → factory, done
- Fail = dull *thud* → plastic (surprising for 1991-09 build, not urgent)
- Time: 5 min

### 2. Exhaust center silencer inspection

- Photo survey manifold → tailpipe, both banks (cats, downpipes, mid-pipe, center silencer, rear silencer, hangers, heat shields)
- Push-test each component by hand, cold; listen for internal rattle at the **center silencer** (resonance source from Apr 19 drive)
- Probe visible shell crack (driver's-side lower portion) with a screwdriver tip:
  - **Crumbles → replace silencer** (~€80–250 part + €80–150 install, or ~€250 all-in)
  - **Solid → weld-patch viable** at a local shop (~€50–150)
- Check hanger + heat-shield integrity whole run
- Output: quote-ready photo set for Ristimaa Oulu or similar
- Time: 30–45 min

### 3. Manual rear ADS level-valve test

- Full procedure: `work/ads_diagnostic/level_control_system.md` line 139
- Engine running, system pressurised
- Disconnect the plastic linkage from the ARB to valve **A 129 320 00 58**
- Manually move the lever to "car is low" (simulate ARB rotation under load)
- **Rear rises** → valve + hydraulic supply OK, problem is upstream (linkage / ARB / switch logic)
- **No rise** → pump pressure / sphere / internal leak suspect
- Outcome updates `work/ads_diagnostic/README.md`
- Time: 30–45 min

### 4. ATF drain & fill cycle #1

- Full procedure: `work/baseline_service/README.md` §F
- **Warm transmission first** (short drive)
- Drain plug: 5 mm hex, new copper seal, torque **15 Nm**
- Refill ~5 L MB 236.1 through dipstick tube (use long funnel)
- Start engine in Park, cycle P-R-N-D-3-2-1 each 3 s, return to Park
- Level check at ~80 °C in Park, engine idling — between upper/lower marks
- Cycles #2–3 wait; #1 does most of the work (expect noticeable coast-down shift improvement)
- Time: 45 min

### 5. Rear diff surface-rust treatment

- **DROP THIS FIRST if Saturday runs long.** Purely preventive from Apr 2 katsastus.
- Wire-brush visible rust on differential housing, wipe dust, brush on Hammerite or zinc primer
- Time: 30 min

### Rest of Sunday: MDF enclosure build (4–6 h)

Full spec: `work/subwoofer_enclosure/README.md` (cut list, assembly order, sealing, polyfill, terminal cup wiring)

Realistic Sunday stop: **carcass sealed and drying, terminal cup wired, driver NOT yet mounted** (wait for silicone cure before seating gasket).

Critical non-obvious point: **DVC2 coils must stay electrically isolated at the terminal cup** — each coil to its own DSP channel (Ch 5 + Ch 6). Paralleling kills the per-coil thermal load-balancing the UP 6DSP is designed for.
