## Sunday — Audio focus: sub box build + (optimistic) DSP power & BE2210 tap

### Block A (priority): MDF enclosure build (4–6 h)

Full spec: `work/subwoofer_enclosure/README.md` — geometry locked Saturday evening (single-axis-tapered + corner-cut, 7 panels + 4 cleat strips, ~16.9 L external / ~12.5 L effective).

Sunday stop point depends on cure windows:
- Optimistic: **carcass sealed and drying, terminal cup wired, driver NOT yet mounted** (silicone needs ≥24 h before driver gasket seats).
- Realistic if shopping or panel-saw queue eats the morning: **carcass dry-fitted and pilot-drilled**, glue-up Monday evening.

Critical non-obvious point: **DVC2 coils must stay electrically isolated at the terminal cup** — each coil to its own DSP channel (Ch 5 + Ch 6). Paralleling kills the per-coil thermal load-balancing the UP 6DSP is designed for.

### Block B (stretch goal — only if Block A finishes with daylight + energy left): in-car DSP power + BE2210 high-level tap

Replaces the previously-planned bench test. Reasoning: with the box drying and the console already needing to come out for the BE2210 tap on a future weekend anyway, doing it now lets us bench-test against the *real* car instead of dummy wiring. If we abort Block B partway, no harm done — just leave the cables coiled and the BE2210 reinstalled.

Sub-steps (in order; stop at any natural break-point if energy fades):

1. **DSP power wiring (battery → passenger-side cubby).** Per `docs/parts_to_order.md` Priority 6B and `work/audio_upgrade_blueprint.md` §1.
   - Biltema 84-574 CCA 8 mm² kit. Hex-crimped lugs, dielectric grease on ring terminals, 40 A AGU fuse within ~30 cm of the battery + post.
   - **DSP location: passenger-side rear cubby** (NOT driver-side — sub box fully consumes the driver-side cubby per the locked geometry).
   - Re-torque check noted for the 3-month and 12-month diary entries (CCA creep mitigation).
2. **Center console out.** Per `work/center_console_refresh/README.md` Phase 1 (steps 1.1–1.8). Open both rear cubbies for cable access.
3. **BE2210 high-level tap.** Per `work/center_console_refresh/README.md` Phase 4 (steps 4.1–4.6). One of the 3 m shielded CAT6 runs — Wago 221-413 lever nuts at the ISO block, shield grounded at DSP end only.
4. **Cubby-to-cubby sub speaker leg.** Per `work/center_console_refresh/README.md` Phase 5.7b (NEW). 2 × 2 m of 2.5 mm² OFC speaker cable, passenger-side cubby → driver-side cubby via under-bulkhead-carpet route (preferred) or through-the-console route (acceptable fallback).
5. **Smoke test the live signal path** before reinstalling the console wood:
   - DSP powers on at the AGU fuse insert.
   - BE2210 in DIN, tuned to a known FM station, volume up: DSP auto-wakes within 1 s.
   - Multimeter on AC volts at the DSP high-level input ferrule ends: 0.1–1 V AC tracks BE2210 volume.
   - If sub box has cured enough to risk a quick tap-test, attach the cubby-to-cubby leg to the (still-loose) terminal cup and confirm the cone moves on a bass beat. Otherwise leave the speaker leg coiled at the driver-side cubby.

If Block B has to abort mid-stream: the absolute minimum safe abort is "console wood reinstalled, BE2210 reinstalled, AGU fuse pulled." Anything else (coiled cables, un-terminated speaker leg) is fine to leave for the next session.
